The short version

Dumpling Jumper collects no data. The app has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. On the App Store privacy label it is declared as Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

The app keeps one small settings record on your device: the sound setting, the chosen language, your local high scores and duel record, the coin bank, unlocked skins, the daily-tower streak and which achievements have already been reported. This record never leaves the device through anything we operate. It may be included in your device backup according to your Apple settings, and deleting the app removes it.

Game Center, if you are signed in

The daily tower leaderboard and the achievements use Apple's Game Center. If your device is signed in to Game Center, the app submits your scores, heights and unlocked achievements to Apple, where they are shown to you and, on the leaderboards, to other players under your Game Center nickname. This is Apple's service and Apple's privacy policy applies; Connectix GmbH never receives your name, email, Apple ID or any other personal detail from it. If you are not signed in, or decline the sign-in sheet, the game plays exactly the same and simply skips the leaderboards. You can sign out at any time in the iOS Settings app under Game Center.

Purchases

The Kitchen Pass and the tip jar are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is owned. We receive no payment details and no identity. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing

The daily results card has a share button. It composes a short text block with your tower number, height, score, combo and streak and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a recipient, and what you send is then subject to that messaging service's own policy.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only connections are Apple's: the App Store for purchases and Game Center for scores, both only when you use those features. There are no third-party SDKs; the app links Apple frameworks only.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to disclose under children's privacy rules. Purchases are subject to the parental controls of the App Store.

This website

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that collects data, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
