Help & support.

Dumpling Jumper is an arcade climber for iPhone and iPad. We answer every message to info@elorenz.ch within 24 hours.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to steer the monkey; it bounces on its own. Hold while falling and the whole kitchen slows down (steam time), which gives you a moment to line up a landing. Land dead centre on a dumpling for a PERFECT and a bigger bounce. Falling out of the bottom of the steamer costs a heart; run out of hearts and the run ends.

What do the dumplings do?

Yellow is the plain bounce. Gold catapults you and rains coins. Silver kicks you sideways and gives you a shield. Red fires five homing chillies at the wasps and robots. Blue freezes every enemy so you can bounce off them. Pink heals a heart and pulls coins toward you. Rainbow launches you straight up. Glass shatters after one bounce. Land on three of the same colour in a row and the skewer on the side fills up: three of a kind sets off that colour's super move.

What is the scent meter?

Fly through the steam above a dumpling and the meter on the left fills. Full meter means MUNCH MODE: for a few seconds your monkey bites an extra chunk out of every dumpling it lands on, for bonus points.

How does two-player work?

Pick 2 players on the menu. Each player gets a tower of their own built from the same seed, so you both climb the identical course. On iPhone, and on iPad held upright, the towers stack; on iPad in landscape they sit side by side. Face to face is the default seating: the far tower is turned around so the device can lie flat on the table between you, and everyone drags on their own tower. Switch to side-by-side in the menu if you are both looking from the same side. When a player runs out of hearts they leave their tower and the other keeps climbing; the higher score wins.

What is the daily tower?

One course per day, identical on every device in the world, so your height is directly comparable to a friend's. The day changes at midnight UTC. Playing it once a day keeps a streak going, with a coin bonus at 3 and 7 days and every seventh day after. Daily runs do not touch your regular high scores. The results card has a share button that composes a short text with your tower number, height, score, combo and streak.

Where are the leaderboards?

The daily tower and the all-time boards live in Game Center. The app signs you in silently if your device already is; if not, iOS shows the Game Center sign-in sheet once at launch. If you skipped it, sign in via the iOS Settings app under Game Center and relaunch. Without Game Center everything else plays normally; only the leaderboard button is hidden.

How do I unlock skins?

Coins you collect in a run are banked when the run ends, and the bank persists. Open the shop (the monkey button) and spend coins on any of the six skins. In a duel each player can wear a different skin. The Kitchen Pass unlocks all six at once for both players; it is purely cosmetic and changes nothing about how the game plays. The tip jar is a voluntary thank-you and grants nothing.

I bought the Kitchen Pass on another device

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. The pass supports Family Sharing, so family members can restore it too. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language?

The language picker sits on the title screen. German, English, French, Italian and Simplified Chinese can be switched at any time, without restarting, and the choice is remembered.

No sound?

Tap the music button in the bottom row (or in the strip between the towers in a duel). If the phone's silent switch is on, the game still plays audio, so check the volume buttons. Sound is prepared while the title screen is showing; a phone call or Siri may pause it and it resumes on return.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Delete the app and it is gone. Scores you submitted to Game Center are held by Apple and can be managed from your Apple ID.
