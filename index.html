It smells like dumplings

A pogo monkey.
A tower of dumplings.

Dumpling Jumper is an arcade climber for iPhone and iPad. A pogo monkey bounces up an endless tower of dumplings inside a giant bamboo steamer, and the higher you get, the louder the kitchen gets.

Every dumpling bounces differently

Gold catapults you and rains coins. Silver kicks you sideways and hands you a shield. Red fires five homing chillies. Blue freezes the wasps into springboards. Glass shatters into a shower of shards. Land dead centre for a PERFECT and the combo climbs with you. Thread three of a colour onto the skewer and something enormous happens.

Fly through the scent above a dumpling and the meter fills. Fill it completely and MUNCH MODE kicks in: your monkey bites a chunk out of every dumpling it lands on.

Two players, one device

Each player gets a tower of their own, built from the same seed, so the course is identical dumpling for dumpling and the only variable is the two of you. On iPhone the towers stack; on iPad in landscape they sit side by side. Lay the device flat between you and the far tower turns around, so you play face to face across a table.

The daily tower

One course per day, the same on every device on Earth. Climb it, keep a streak going, compare your height on the Game Center leaderboard, and share your result as a text block that quotes cleanly in any messenger.

No ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes. Six monkey skins, every one earnable with coins from play. Plays in German, English, French, Italian and Simplified Chinese, switchable inside the game.
